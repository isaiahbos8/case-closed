# Case Closed

Case Closed fills in your Excel budget from a bank statement. Upload a statement, swipe right on each purchase to approve it, and the app adds those purchases to the budget you already use.

- **Website:** https://isaiahbos8.github.io/case-closed/
- **App:** https://isaiahbos8.github.io/case-closed/case-closed-app.html
- **Tester guide:** https://isaiahbos8.github.io/case-closed/guide.html

Everything runs in your browser. Your budget file and statements are read on your device and never uploaded. The app saves an updated copy of your budget and never changes the original.

A student project for MGMT 342 at Xavier University.
