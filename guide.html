Case Closed

Thanks for testing Case Closed.

Case Closed updates an Excel budget from a bank statement. You swipe each purchase to approve it, and the app writes it into the spreadsheet. You'll try it with a made-up student named Jordan, so you don't need any of your own money info.

About 5 minutesUse your phoneNothing of yours needed

What to do

  1. Open the appUse the button below. A computer works too.Open Case Closed
  2. Swipe the three practice cardsThey're on the start screen. Swipe right to approve, left to skip, and watch the rows appear below them.
  3. Tap "See how it works"Do Jordan's November update: look at the budget, add Jordan's card statement, and swipe through the purchases.
  4. Look at the budget at the endThe bars should have moved. If you have Excel, you can download Jordan's file and find the new rows on the Spending Tracker tab.

Yellow boxes tell you what to tap next. Try to get through it without asking for help. Where you get stuck is exactly what we want to know.
