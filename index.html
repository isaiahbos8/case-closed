What it won't do

It records. It never touches your money.

No bank login

It never connects to your bank account. You bring the statements and receipts.

No moving money

It never pays, transfers or reimburses anything. It only writes down amounts.

No sending your files

Receipts and statements are read on your phone. Only the purchases you approve go into your Excel file.

Questions

Before you try it.

Do I need to be good at Excel?+

No. The app reads the categories and amounts already in your budget and fills in the rows for you. You approve purchases on your phone and never have to type into a cell.

Is my bank information safe?+

The app never logs into your bank. Your receipts and statements are read on your phone and stay there. The only thing it sends is each purchase you approve, written as a row into your own Excel file on OneDrive.

What if my receipt and my bank show different amounts?+

The card shows both amounts, for example “Receipt $14.20” and “Bank $16.70,” and you tap the right one. Nothing goes in until you pick.

What can I add?+

Receipt photos and screenshots, bank or card statements saved as CSV or PDF, receipt emails, and purchases you type in yourself.
